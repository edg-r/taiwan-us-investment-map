About these figures

State color is the documented subtotal for 2014 to 5 Nov 2026. Larger amounts are deeper plum in the light theme and brighter plum in the dark theme. A hatched state has records but no documented total for the active investor type. A plain gray state has no records in the ledger. Neither blank means zero.

The documented subtotal sums sourced, state-specific project commitments, completed project totals, acquisitions, equity investments and loan financing. It is not foreign direct investment (FDI) stock, an annual investment flow, or a verified transfer from Taiwan. Announced scope does not establish how much was spent. Unallocated deals and presence-only records are excluded from the subtotal.

Sources include company releases and reports, state and local government announcements, regulatory filings and public records. Each record links to its source. Full project scopes are counted once; missing state amounts remain blank. This is a documented ledger, not a complete census of Taiwanese investment. Click a company to see its other U.S. states.

Read the largest figures with care

Full project scopes enter the subtotal once. TSMC’s superseded $12.00 billion, $40.00 billion, $65.00 billion and $165.00 billion announcements are not added to its latest $265.00 billion Arizona program. Joint ventures use the documented project scope, with any separately disclosed Taiwanese investor commitment retained separately and never added again. National and unallocated values stay outside state totals. Cancelled or sold records have null investment amounts, with historical figures retained as transaction values. Grants, incentives and economic impact estimates are excluded. Null means unknown or excluded, never zero.

Judgment calls

Formosa Sunshine’s $9.40 billion Louisiana project remains in the total as paused: a 29 September 2026 federal court order still calls it proposed, rather than cancelled. Formosa Point Comfort’s $5.00 billion program is excluded because it was announced in 2012, before the window. Foxconn Wisconsin uses the November 2025 amended $1.20 billion target; the original 2017 $10.00 billion plan is retained as transaction value. Foxconn Lordstown was sold to a SoftBank affiliate in 2025 and has a null investment amount. CPC Corporation’s up to $4.40 billion Alaska LNG investment is planned under a letter of intent, not a final investment decision, and is classified as government.

Government investors can include the National Development Fund, CPC Corporation, Taipower, Bureau of Labor Funds and Taiwan Eximbank. Loans or guarantees already covered by a firm project are excluded from additive totals.

Investor type

Each record names its investor type. Firms are commercial companies, including their U.S. affiliates and joint ventures. Government covers public institutions, state-owned enterprises, public lenders, sovereign and public pension funds, and government frameworks, as classified in the ledger. The type describes the investor. It does not verify where the money came from.

All, Firms and Government filter every state figure, color, record list, tooltip and flow. The choice is kept in the link, for example #type=government, and combines with a company link such as #type=firm&company=tsmc. Downloads always include both types.

National records

The January 2026 U.S.-Taiwan investment memorandum of understanding (MOU) covers $250.00 billion of corporate investment plus up to $250.00 billion in credit guarantees. These are non-additive with each other and with state records. The February 2026 Agreement on Reciprocal Trade references that framework and industrial clusters; it creates no additional investment tranche here. Corporate references also overlap: TSMC’s $265.00 billion pledge is the Arizona program, and GlobalWafers’ $7.50 billion pledge overlaps Sherman. All eight national records remain separate, without a combined national total, and never enter state colors or flows.
